ZODUFAX - Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

ZODUFAX collects minimal data to provide its services. We may collect: device identifiers for advertising purposes, gameplay data such as scores and streaks stored locally on your device, and anonymous usage statistics.

2. Advertising

ZODUFAX uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized ads. You can purchase the "No Ads" option to remove all advertisements permanently.

3. Firebase

We use Firebase for real-time multiplayer features (Duel mode) and push notifications. Firebase may collect anonymous usage data to improve app performance.

4. In-App Purchases

ZODUFAX offers in-app purchases processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not store your payment information.

5. Data Storage

Your game progress, XP, streaks, and preferences are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

ZODUFAX is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
